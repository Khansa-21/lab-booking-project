Privacy policy

Your data and privacy

University Resource Hub uses account and booking information only to provide resource booking, approval, training, and check-in services.

This page is a project privacy policy. A university using this system should review and replace it with its official policy and contact details.

Information we store

  • Name, email address, account role, and password hash.
  • Bookings, waitlist entries, training records, approvals, and check-in records.
  • Essential login cookie information used to keep you signed in.

Why we use it

We use this information to manage shared resources, prevent booking conflicts, confirm eligibility, and support fair access.

Where it is processed

The application uses Vercel for the frontend, Azure App Service for the backend, Azure SQL Database for stored data, and Brevo for password-reset emails.

Your choices

Ask your university resource administrator if you need to correct account information, ask about your records, or report a privacy concern.
