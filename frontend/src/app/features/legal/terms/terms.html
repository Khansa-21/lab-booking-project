Terms of use

Use resources responsibly

This system is for booking approved university resources. Users must give correct information and follow their university's equipment and laboratory rules.

These are project terms. A university using the system should review them and add its official equipment, safety, and cancellation rules.

Booking rules

  • Book only resources you are allowed and trained to use.
  • Arrive on time and check in when required.
  • Cancel a booking when you no longer need it.
  • Do not attempt to create overlapping or false reservations.

No-shows and access

A booking without check-in can be marked as a no-show after the allowed grace period. Staff may approve, reject, or manage restricted-resource bookings.

Equipment care

Use university equipment safely and report damage, faults, or missing items to the resource administrator.

Account security

Keep your password private. Contact your university resource administrator if you believe someone has accessed your account.
